## Basic Data and Setup
- **Date:** 2026-08-23
- **Location:** Bauhaus Parking Lot
- **Goal:** Evaluation of driving behavior following modifications to the suspension and steering components.
- **Background:** The shock absorbers were filled with #1000 shock oil and spring preload was increased. A Konect X9S remote control and a Savöx servo were installed to increase steering precision. The steering exponential curve was set to linear. The C-Hub screws were loosened to minimize mechanical resistance and ensure an accurate return of the servo to the neutral position.

## Results
- The vehicle exhibits increased stability.
- Corrective steering maneuvers during the run can be executed effectively.
- The vehicle does not maintain constant contact with the road surface. This is attributed to a potentially faulty geometry setup and surface irregularities on the test track. These irregularities exceed the physical capabilities of the light 1:10 scale Carten T410R chassis.
- The driving skills of the pilot are insufficient.

## Measures
- Driving practice must be intensified.
- A test track with smoother asphalt and without curbs or other obstacles must be identified.
- The suspension geometry and weight distribution must be analyzed and adjusted.
