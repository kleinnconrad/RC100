# Error log and resolutions

| No. | Date | error | resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | smoketest / 2026-03-17 | **ESC punch too aggressive:** The factory punch level (level 5) proved to be too hard for the long gear ratio. This made starting difficult and led to ESC emergency shutdowns during the 1st test drive. | Procurement of a programming card. The punch value was reduced to level 1. In addition, the vehicle is pushed slightly when starting. |
| 2 | 2026-03-17 | **total loss of gearbox:** The motor pinion slipped forward due to an insufficiently fixed grub screw. The asymmetrical engagement destroyed the plastic main gear and deformed the pinion bore. | Installation of a new 48DP motor pinion made of hardened steel (5 mm bore) and a kimbrough 72T upgrade main gear. The pinion was secured with medium-strength threadlocker (Loctite). |
| 3 | 2026-03-17 | **loss of locking pins:** Due to the high speeds (> 25,000 RPM) in the speed test, the O-rings of the cup joints slipped. As a result, the locking pins were thrown out. | For the future, a more flexible and stable fixation of the locking pins is being evaluated to withstand the rotational forces. |
| 4 | 2026-03-17 | **wrong motor polarity:** The set technical forward gear on the remote control corresponded to the physical reverse gear. | The motor polarity was reversed and the mechanical endpoints of the ESC were retaught to the remote control. |
| 5 | 2026-03-26 | **loosening of the wheel suspension:** The screw connection of the ball joint on the rear left wheel suspension loosened completely during the 3rd test drive. | All ball joint screw connections on the wishbones were dismantled and secured with liquid threadlocker (Loctite). Metal screws were tightened. |
| 6 | 01.04. - 16.04.2026 | **spongy front end behavior / dancing rear:** At increasing speeds, the front axle showed a lack of guidance. The unlocked differential and the rear weight favored instability. | Iterative optimization: 1. Additional weight on the front bumper (up to 175 g). 2. Installation of horseshoe springs for a harder servo saver. 3. Symmetrical chassis geometry: Camber set to -1.5°, toe-in to 1.5° and steeper wishbone angles. |
| 7 | 2026-04-03 | **rear-end collision:** During the 7th test drive, an accident occurred in which the rear right wheel suspension was mechanically damaged. | Disassembly of the chassis and complete replacement of the defective components on the rear right wheel suspension. |
| 8 | 2026-04-16 | **collision with driver:** A loss of control during a speedrun (9th test drive) resulted in an impact against the driver's foot. | Determination to conduct future high-speed tests exclusively on significantly larger and obstacle-free asphalt areas. |
| 9 | 2026-04-19 | **displacement of the bevel gear:** After the 1st speedrun session, the bevel gear of the front driveshaft mount had migrated by approx. 2 mm on the shaft (presumably breakage of the locking pin). | The bevel gear was pushed back completely onto the shaft with a positive fit, screwed tight and the screw connection secured with superglue. |
| 10 | 2026-04-19 | **driveshaft in resonance:** Due to the high speeds, the driveshaft started vibrating (whipping) and caused contact on the servo mount (already observed in the speed test). | No mechanical interventions were made, as the shaft remained undamaged and the contact was classified as uncritical. |
| 11 | 2026-04-19 | **pushed out ball bearing:** A ball bearing on the front differential housing was pushed half out of its seat by the occurring loads. | The condition was initially rated as technically uncritical, as no blockage or consequential damage occurred. |
| 12 | 2026-07-19 | **instability during strong acceleration:** Incorrect chassis tuning limited the speed. Causes: Completely blocked anti-roll bars, asymmetrical wheel camber in front and asymmetrical toe-in in rear. | The anti-roll bars were completely removed. The chassis geometry on the front and rear axles was set exactly symmetrically. |
| 13 | 2026-07-26 | **destabilization by wind influence:** Upcoming wind led to the destabilization of the vehicle and resulted in a crash. The battery compartment was damaged. A remaining residual restlessness also prevented the full utilization of the vehicle's performance. | Test runs will be carried out exclusively in calm conditions in the future. A spool is installed. |
| 14 | 2026-08-22 | **inaccurate steering neutral return:** Excessive tightening of the C-hub mounting screws created mechanical resistance. This prevented the steering mechanism from moving freely, causing the servo to fail to return precisely to the neutral position. | The mounting screws of the C-hubs were loosened to eliminate the mechanical resistance. |
